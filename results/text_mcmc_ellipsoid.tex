We sample the posteriors with NUTS \citep{Hoffman2014} in {\sc numpyro} \citep{Phan2019}, four chains of 1\,000 draws after 1\,500 warm-up steps (500 for the RR Lyrae fit at $4<\rho<9^\circ$). Every run has $\hat R\leq1.017$ and at least 217 effective draws per parameter; one stage-1 fit (giants, $-1.5$ to $-1.1$) has 5 divergent transitions among its 4\,000 draws, the others none.
